Privacy Policy — Carnet de Tir

Last updated: 27th August 2026

1. Data Collected

Carnet de Tir is a sport shooting results tracking application. The
data you enter (shooters, match results, weapon settings, arrow sets,
etc.) is stored locally on your device.

2. Dropbox Synchronization

If you enable the synchronization feature, the application requests
permission to access a private, dedicated folder within your Dropbox
account (App Folder access only). This folder is not visible or
accessible from the rest of your Dropbox account, and is used solely
to store a backup copy of your application data.

The developer of Carnet de Tir never has access to the data stored in
your Dropbox account. Only the application, with your authorization,
can read and write to this dedicated folder.

3. Use of Data

The data collected and synchronized is used exclusively to:
- Ensure backup of your results and settings,
- Allow data restoration in case of a device change or app
  reinstallation.

No data is shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising
purposes.

4. Data Deletion

You may disable synchronization at any time from the application's
settings. You may also revoke the application's access to your
Dropbox account at any time from your Dropbox account's connected
apps settings (dropbox.com/account/connected_apps).

Deleting the application from your device removes all locally stored
data. Deleting the synchronization file from your Dropbox can be done
by revoking the application's access as described above.
